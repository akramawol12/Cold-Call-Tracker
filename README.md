# 📞 Cold Call Challenge Tracker

An offline-first, ADHD-optimized outbound dialing dashboard and CRM designed to keep outbound sales agents engaged, stimulated, and focused. By transforming mundane prospecting into a gamified challenge, this workspace mitigates understimulation while providing direct utility for managing lead outreach.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🧠 1. ADHD Focus Booster Hub (Sensory Integration)
Designed specifically for high-distraction environments to align understimulated attention spans:
*   **Tactile Fidget Release Clicker:** Discharge physical hand tension or physical restlessness directly in the browser. Features a satisfying micro-click mechanism that boosts your Dopamine XP bar.
*   **Web-Audio Sensory Synthesizer:** Direct real-time audio chimes providing instant audio rewards—crystalline pitch bubble pings on contact logs, negative clicks, and majestic chords on successful conversions.
*   **2-Minute Dial Blitz Sprint:** Micro-sprint timers to setting rapid 120-second deadlines per list action, boosting focus and preventing chronic overthinking.
*   **Dopamine XP Level & Streak Counter:** High-contrast visual markers showing your current dial streaks and live level-up tracks.

### 🇪🇹 2. Ethiopian Traditional & East Africa (EAT) Clock
Built with native time-zone awareness for outbound teams coordinating local operations in or with East Africa:
*   **Real-time UTC+3 Addis Ababa Sync:** Accurate tracking for business hours.
*   **Ethio-Time Cycle Format:** Automatically displays the traditional cyclical 12-hour clock (starting at 6:00 AM sunrise) using traditional **ቀን (Day)** and **ማታ (Night)** periods.

### 📊 3. Streamlined Prospect Dialer & CRM pipeline
*   **Blitz List Creator:** Effortlessly dump lead matrices, corporate targets, and priority numbers.
*   **Categorization Funnels:** Move leads along a pipeline sector (**Unlisted** 💡, **Interested** 👍, **Closed/Deal Won** 🎉, **Rejected** 👎) with intuitive visual actions.
*   **Live Contacts Directories:** Access complete interaction history, dates, and timestamped logs recorded directly in EAT standard time.

### 💡 4. Hourly Focus Fuel
*   An interactive banner generating fresh motivational quotes updating on the hour with a visual countdown detailing exactly when the next hourly quote refresh will launch.
